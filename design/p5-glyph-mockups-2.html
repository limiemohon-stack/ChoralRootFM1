P5 glyph mock-ups, round 2

Every tile is one cell of the editor at the FM-1's own pixels (60 wide; the glyph box 34 high), enlarged 3x with hard pixels. Left: the cell on the active row (its knob colour). Right: the same cell on a dim row. Lines are 2 px or more everywhere.

Knob: K6 with the eleven ticks

The face tinted in the row colour, a white 3 px pointer; what varies is the ticks (the scale that matches a real Prophet) and the tint.

Button: B1 with the cap in the column colour

The 6 px red LED with its dark halo when on, a dark-red dot when off; the icon 4 px above. What varies is how the cap carries the colour.

Three-way switch: the P5 cap with two LEDs (Key trk: Off / Half / Full)

A wide cap in the column colour with two LEDs. Two codings: true binary (00 · 01 · 10) and a count (none · one · both).
