Chord Layout (Chord Keys): Classic and FM-1

The design in docs/CHORD-LAYOUT.md, decided 2026-10-16. Screens at the FM-1's 240 x 240 pixels, enlarged 2x; the keybeds are diagrams of the 27 keys with each key's light at its level and the top row of buttons.

Key lights: litmiddleglow faintoff Labels: types · extensions · LOCK · roots

1. The two layouts

Classic is today's (the Orchid's). FM-1: the three small black keys are the types, SUS is G#3 + A#3 with one finger, B3 is the 9, LOCK moves to LFO, C4 and C#4 are roots (20 instead of 18).

2. The Options

The owner's names (second round): Chord Keys after Secret Chords, SUS Window after it (listed only in FM-1), Full K Shorts after Split Point, MIDI Keys after MIDI Raw Chord. All four automatic; hidden on the SMK-37.

3. FM-1: LOCK on LFO, LATCH on GLO held + LFO (Classic unchanged)

LFO tap: LOCK on / off (the LFO light lit, steady). LFO held: the LOCK layer (LFO blinks as an open layer's button). GLO held, then LFO: LATCH (LFO held, then GLO: the LOCK layer; GLO held alone never opens the Options, only a quick click does); while LATCH is on the LFO light pulses (lit, middle, glow, middle, 300 ms each); with LOCK on as well it pulses between lit and middle.

4. SUS latched, and the combine window

A latched SUS shows as two lit keys, G#3 and A#3; the same pair pressed again on its own switches it off at the release. The SUS Window: a root pressed while it is open waits for the decision (the pair, or the window's close), then the chord plays once.

5. The Full keyboard with the shortcuts on, and the FM-1 panel picture

F3 .. F5 are notes (25). F#5: the layout's chord keys on the low keys, the roots ending at F5; G5: a chord on every key (the latched chord, else Key Mode's). Both toggle on a quick press, switch only while held on a long one. KNOB 1 is the voicing on the Full keyboard, in both layouts.
