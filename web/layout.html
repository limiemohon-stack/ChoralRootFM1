Layout designer

steps 1 to 4 of 5: the designer The sound editor's pages drawn by the firmware's own renderer (the whole firmware, compiled to WebAssembly, on its real engines and factory sounds). Viewer: today's pages as the unit shows them (P5: the owner's rows, compiled from p5-rows.json). Design: rearrange today's pages or lay out new free screens, and export the design as JSON. Every screen, free ones too, is drawn and turned by the firmware's own editor and renderer, as the unit would. A design is compiled into the firmware build (tools/gen_layout.py), never loaded by a unit; a screen the build would refuse (a flat free screen, a band, an element off the grid ..) says why.
