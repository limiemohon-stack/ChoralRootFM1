# ChoralRoot, the VA chord's voices on two cores (HISTORY 3y177; docs/VA.md "Two cores"): tools/emu/test_cr.sh runs
# this on a fresh unit (Options > Dual Core On: the emulator's core 1 runs its job inline), again with EMU_C1=thread
# (the job, core 1's share of the chord's voices with it, on a second thread: the same WAV), EMU_C1=nostart (one
# core: the same WAV, every voice on core 0) and EMU_C1=hang (core 1 given up in the first block: one core's WAV).
# The unit's heaviest VA pair (tools/perf_unit.py va): the chord part on 21 MORPH PAD (two MORPH oscillators,
# SPREAD: a stereo side), the bass part on 18 PUNCH BASS; eight notes over MIDI (perf_unit.py's maj13 voicing), a bass
# note under them, the chord moved up a semitone (eight steals), released.
wait 300
btn GLO down
wait 100
knob PRESETS +2               # OPT + PRESETS: the chord part (FM6 -> FM TONE) to the VA (01 LUSH PAD), on OPT's release
wait 100
btn GLO up
wait 50
knob PRESETS +20              # VA 21 MORPH PAD
wait 300
knob ALGORITHM +19            # the bass part (the VA, DEEP SUB): OFF -> VA 18 PUNCH BASS
wait 300
dump                          # 1: MORPH PAD, PUNCH BASS, no voice
midi 90 30 64                 # channel 1 (CHORD): C3 E3 G3 B3 D4 F#4 A4 C5
midi 90 34 64
midi 90 37 64
midi 90 3B 64
midi 90 3E 64
midi 90 42 64
midi 90 45 64
midi 90 48 64
wait 1200
dump                          # 2: eight voices
midi 91 24 64                 # channel 2 (BASS): C2
wait 1200
dump                          # 3: the chord eight, the bass one
midi 80 30 00                 # the chord a semitone up: every voice taken for a new note
midi 80 34 00
midi 80 37 00
midi 80 3B 00
midi 80 3E 00
midi 80 42 00
midi 80 45 00
midi 80 48 00
midi 90 31 64
midi 90 35 64
midi 90 38 64
midi 90 3C 64
midi 90 3F 64
midi 90 43 64
midi 90 46 64
midi 90 49 64
wait 1500
dump                          # 4: eight voices still, the bass one
midi 80 31 00
midi 80 35 00
midi 80 38 00
midi 80 3C 00
midi 80 3F 00
midi 80 43 00
midi 80 46 00
midi 80 49 00
midi 81 24 00
wait 6000
dump                          # 5: none
quit
