# P5, the owner's gesture (HISTORY 3y44, docs/PROPHET.md "The crackle"): "preset 16 .. minor + 6 + 9 then playing chord
# again as minor + 9 which had a long crackling effect like the voice got stuck in a washing machine .. All dry, no bass
# on" (his build: all 200 programs, so place 16 = Sequential's program 16, CANNED HAPPY).
# tools/emu/test_cr.sh writes the place into @STEPS@ (PRESETS turns from place 01): program 16's place in this build's
# shipped list, or, where the list does not ship it, place 15. The chord keys: G#3 = MIN, F3 = 6, C4 = 9; the root D4:
# Dm6/9 = 62 65 69 71 76, five notes on P5's four voices (voice.c: the second from the bottom gives way); then the 6 is
# let go and the root struck again 100 ms after its release (Dm9 = 62 65 69 76 over four voices still releasing: the 65
# has no voice and waits for a fade), and once more 30 ms after. Dumps: 1 the sound; 2 the five-note chord held; 3, 4
# the four-note chord held; 5 six seconds after the last release: no voice left. The strikes are at 3.16, 4.76 and
# 6.29 s of the script's clock.
# test_cr.sh runs it twice: as it is (--wav: no jump, no hole, no burst inside the sound), and with EMU_SHED_OVER=4
# (README "Overload": a unit that does not keep up with four voices): the shed fades a voice of each chord and that
# is all it does.
keys classic                  # (0.25.1: FM-1 is the default; this script was written for the Classic chord keys)
wait 300
btn GLO down
wait 100
knob PRESETS +4               # OPT + PRESETS: P5
wait 200
btn GLO up
wait 500
knob PRESETS +@STEPS@
wait 500
btn FX down                   # "All dry": the FX layer's knobs, chorus, delay and reverb down to 0
wait 500
knob KNOB2 -12
wait 100
knob KNOB3 -8
wait 100
knob KNOB4 -10
wait 100
btn FX up
wait 300
btn OCT-
wait 300
dump                          # 1
btn G#3 down
btn F3 down
btn C4 down
wait 60
btn D4 down
wait 1500
expect sound
dump                          # 2
btn F3 up
btn D4 up
wait 100
btn D4 down
wait 1500
expect sound
dump                          # 3
btn D4 up
wait 30
btn D4 down
wait 1500
expect sound
dump                          # 4
btn D4 up
btn G#3 up
btn C4 up
wait 6000
dump                          # 5
