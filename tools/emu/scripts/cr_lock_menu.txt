# ChoralRoot: the LOCK layer (the owner, 2026-10-10): B3 held past the hold time opens it (a B3 tap is still LOCK on /
# off, in the layer too; a hold toggles nothing); KNOB 1 "Type key" Clears / Keeps, KNOB 2 "Ext keys" Toggle / Pushed;
# OCT-, OCT+, HOME and B3 held again close it; the roots and the chord keys play in it.
#   chord block: F3 6, F#3 DIM, G3 m7, G#3 MIN, A3 M7, A#3 MAJ, B3 LOCK, C4 9, C#4 SUS
# The four styles, each from MAJ + 6 + 9 latched (the dumps' "lock:" line: latched = bits DIM 01 MIN 02 MAJ 04 SUS 08,
# 6 10, m7 20, M7 40, 9 80):
#   Clears + Toggle (today's): MIN: the extensions cleared (02); 6: on (12); 6 again: off (02)
#   Keeps + Toggle: MIN: 6 and 9 stay (92); 9 tapped: off (12)
#   Keeps + Pushed, the owner's sequence (m7 is his "7"), from MIN + 6 (12): m7: the set is m7 (22); m7 again, alone,
#                   the only extension on: off (02); m7: on (22); m7 held + 9: both (A2); then m7 alone: m7 (22: two
#                   were on, so a lone push is the set); 9 alone: 9 (82); MAJ: the type alone changes (84); MIN (82)
#   Clears + Pushed: 6 + 9 (92); MAJ: the extensions cleared (04)
keys classic                  # (0.25.1: FM-1 is the default; this script was written for the Classic chord keys)
wait 300
btn B3 600                    # held: the layer; LOCK not toggled
wait 300
expect led OCT- on
shot build/emu/test/cr_lock_menu.ppm
dump                          # 1: layer 10, lock off, clears / toggle
btn B3                        # a tap in the layer: LOCK on
wait 300
dump                          # 2: lock on, layer 10
btn A#3 down                  # MAJ + 6 + 9 pressed together, released: latched (the chord keys keep their job here)
btn F3 down
btn C4 down
wait 100
btn C4 up
btn F3 up
btn A#3 up
wait 200
btn D4 down                   # the roots play in the layer
wait 300
expect sound
dump                          # 3: latched 94, D6/9 sounding
btn D4 up
wait 200
btn G#3                       # Clears: MIN alone: the extensions go
wait 200
dump                          # 4: 02
btn F3                        # Toggle: 6 on
wait 200
dump                          # 5: 12
btn F3                        # .. and off
wait 200
dump                          # 6: 02
btn OCT-                      # closes
wait 300
dump                          # 7: layer 0
btn B3 600                    # open again (LOCK stays on: a hold is no tap)
wait 300
knob KNOB1 +1                 # Type key: Keeps
wait 200
shot build/emu/test/cr_lock_menu_keeps.ppm
btn A#3 down                  # MAJ first (alone: it replaces MIN), then 6 and 9 join
wait 50
btn F3 down
btn C4 down
wait 100
btn C4 up
btn F3 up
btn A#3 up
wait 200
dump                          # 8: 94 (the type replaced by MAJ, 6 9 joined), keeps / toggle, lock on, layer 10
btn G#3                       # Keeps: MIN alone: 6 and 9 stay
wait 200
dump                          # 9: 92
btn C4                        # Toggle: 9 off
wait 200
dump                          # 10: 12
btn OCT+                      # closes too (nothing to confirm)
wait 300
dump                          # 11: layer 0
btn B3 600
wait 300
knob KNOB2 +1                 # Ext keys: Pushed (with Keeps)
wait 200
shot build/emu/test/cr_lock_menu_pushed.ppm
btn G3                        # m7 alone ({6} then 7): a new set, m7 only, the type stays
wait 200
dump                          # 12: 22
btn G3 down                   # m7 again, alone, the only extension on: it sounds on while held ..
wait 150
dump                          # 13: 22 still (decided at the release)
btn G3 up
wait 200
dump                          # 14: 02: off, no extension
btn G3                        # ({} then 7): on
wait 200
dump                          # 15: 22
btn G3 down                   # m7 again, and 9 while it is held: both
wait 100
btn C4 down
wait 100
btn C4 up
btn G3 up
wait 200
dump                          # 16: A2
btn G3                        # ({7, 9} then 7 alone): two were on: the set is m7
wait 200
dump                          # 17: 22
btn C4                        # ({7} then 9 alone): 9
wait 200
dump                          # 18: 82
btn A#3                       # Keeps: MAJ changes the type only
wait 200
dump                          # 19: 84
btn G#3                       # .. and MIN
wait 200
btn B3 600                    # B3 held again: closed
wait 300
dump                          # 20: layer 0, 82, lock still on, keeps / pushed
btn B3 600
wait 300
knob KNOB1 -1                 # Clears + Pushed
wait 200
btn F3 down
wait 50
btn C4 down
wait 100
btn C4 up
btn F3 up
wait 200
dump                          # 21: 92
btn A#3                       # Clears: MAJ alone: the extensions cleared
wait 200
dump                          # 22: 04
knob KNOB2 -1                 # back to the defaults
wait 200
btn HOME                      # HOME closes
wait 300
dump                          # 23: layer 0, clears / toggle
btn B3                        # LOCK off (a tap)
wait 2500
dump                          # 24: lock off, latched 00, silent
